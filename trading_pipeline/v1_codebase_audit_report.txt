The V1 trading pipeline consists of 26 core files organized modularly:

  - matrix_builder.py (141 lines): Dynamic 50x50 correlation across raw returns, binary, and spikes.
  - cross_asset.py (160 lines): Injects lagged returns of top 3 correlated & 3 anti-correlated peers.

MODEL DEFINITIONS MODULE (models/):

VALIDATION & TUNING MODULE (validation/):

  - purged_cv.py (207 lines): Purged walk-forward CV generator with purge/embargo gap, Optuna search.

MONITORING & LOGGING MODULE (monitoring/):

  - drift_detector.py (185 lines): Population Stability Index (PSI) feature drift monitoring.
  - metrics_logger.py (133 lines): Per-company validation metrics logging to JSON.

BACKTESTING & RISK MANAGEMENT MODULE (backtest/):
